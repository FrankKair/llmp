# Dependency Review

Review the project's dependencies and dependency configuration.

Inspect manifests, lockfiles, build files, and dependency-related scripts.

Check:

- Unused or duplicate dependencies
- Missing lockfiles or unconstrained versions
- Direct versus transitive dependencies
- Major version risks and compatibility concerns
- Deprecated or abandoned packages, where evidence is available
- Known security issues, but do not claim current vulnerability status without
  evidence or a security audit
- License or supply-chain concerns
- Dependency update and reproducibility practices

Return:

## Findings

For each issue:

- Severity: High, Medium, or Low
- Dependency and version
- Evidence
- Risk
- Recommended action

## Overall assessment

Give a short assessment of dependency health and the highest-priority action.

If no meaningful issues are found, say so explicitly.
