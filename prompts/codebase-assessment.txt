# Codebase Health Assessment

Assess the engineering health of this codebase.

Project context:
[OPTIONAL: describe the project, stack, scope, and known constraints]

Inspect the code, tests, configuration, dependencies, CI/CD, and documentation.
Base conclusions on evidence. Mention relevant file paths, symbols, or commands.
Do not invent metrics, vulnerabilities, tests, or practices.

Use N/A only when a category does not apply. Use "Not assessed" when evidence
is unavailable, and say what is missing. Do not score missing evidence as zero.

## Scoring

Score each applicable category from 0 to 10:

- 0: absent, broken, or dangerous
- 3: weak and problematic
- 5: adequate but inconsistent
- 7: strong with some gaps
- 10: exemplary and well maintained

Use whole numbers. Explain scores briefly and avoid giving high scores based
on a small sample.

## Categories

Assess:

1. Correctness and reliability
2. Maintainability and readability
3. Architecture and modularity
4. Testing
5. Security
6. Dependencies and supply chain
7. CI/CD and release engineering
8. Observability and operations
9. Performance and scalability
10. Documentation and developer experience

## Output

### Executive summary

Give a concise assessment of the codebase's overall health. Mention the
strongest area, weakest area, and most important risk.

### Critical findings

List urgent security, reliability, data-loss, availability, or operational
issues.

For each finding include:

- Severity: Critical, High, Medium, or Low
- Location
- Problem
- Impact
- Recommended action

If none are found, say so. This does not prove that no vulnerabilities exist.

### Strengths

List the most valuable engineering practices found, with evidence.

### Improvements

List up to 5 worthwhile improvements, ordered by value. Do not fill the list
with low-value suggestions to reach a quota.

For each include:

- Priority: P0, P1, P2, or P3
- Recommendation
- Why it matters
- Suggested first step
- Relevant file or directory

Prefer focused improvements over broad rewrites.
